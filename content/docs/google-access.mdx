SimpleDash asks Google for one permission: read-only access to Google Analytics. It can read the reports GA4 already has, and it can't change, add or delete anything in your Google account.

## The exact permission

When you sign in, Google asks you to allow:

| Permission | What it allows | When it's asked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Scope name="analytics" /> | Read your Google Analytics reports and settings | When you sign in |
| <Scope name="search-console" /> | Read your Search Console data | Only if you connect Search Console (Pro) |

Google also shares your name, email address and profile picture, so we can create your account. Nothing else.

## What SimpleDash can't do

- Change any GA4 setting, add users or delete data.
- Read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google service.
- See individual visitors. GA4's reports are totals ("120 visitors from Google"), and that is all SimpleDash asks for.

## Why does Google call it "sensitive"?

Google labels access to Analytics data as a **sensitive** permission, because analytics data belongs to your business. It isn't a warning about SimpleDash in particular: every app that reads GA4 asks for the same permission. Google checks such apps before they can be used by everyone.

## What we store

We keep the Google access token, **encrypted**, so the dashboard can fetch fresh numbers without asking you to sign in again. We also keep short-lived copies of report totals, so pages open fast. Never anything about individual visitors. The full list is in [Data and security](/docs/data-and-security).

## How to remove access

You can end SimpleDash's access at any time, in either place:

- **In SimpleDash:** Settings → Profile → **Disconnect Google**. This revokes the token at Google and signs you out. Your settings and notes stay, so signing in again restores everything.
- **In your Google account:** go to [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), choose SimpleDash and remove its access.

To delete everything instead, see [Disconnect Google or delete your account](/docs/delete-account).
