SimpleDash has two plans. Free shows all your Google Analytics sites for the last <FreeDays /> days, with no card and no time limit. Pro costs <Price plan="pro" interval="month" /> a month or <Price plan="pro" interval="year" /> a year and adds full history, the Monday email, Google search insights and revenue from Stripe.

There is no trial, because Free doesn't end.

## How billing works

- **Payments go through Stripe.** Stripe is the merchant of record: it charges your card and handles sales tax and VAT. SimpleDash never sees your card details.
- **Monthly or yearly.** Switch at any time in Settings → Plan & billing. Stripe shows the exact amount before you confirm.
- **Launch price.** If you subscribe at today's price, it stays the same for as long as your subscription runs without a break.
- **Cancel any time.** Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to Free.

The full rules, including refunds, are in the [terms](/terms).

## What happens when Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Your sites, notes and settings stay. The dashboard goes back to the last <FreeDays /> days, the Monday email stops, and Google search insights and revenue are hidden until you upgrade again (Search Console and Stripe stay connected).

## Upgrade

Go to Settings → **Plan & billing** and choose monthly or yearly. [Settings and billing](/docs/settings-and-billing) has the details.
