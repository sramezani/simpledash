---
title: What SimpleDash can't do
description: "An honest list of what SimpleDash isn't for: no tracking script, no per-visitor journeys or funnels, no changes to GA4, and no data that GA4 doesn't have."
updated: 2026-10-16
---

SimpleDash is a simple way to read the Google Analytics 4 data you already have. It isn't a replacement for GA4's deeper tools, and it isn't a privacy-first tracker. Here's what it doesn't do, so you can pick the right tool.

## It doesn't track visitors itself

There's no SimpleDash script. If your site doesn't run GA4, SimpleDash has nothing to show. If you want to leave Google Analytics entirely, a tool with its own script, such as Plausible or Fathom, is a better fit. [How they compare](/compare/simpledash-vs-plausible).

## It doesn't show individual visitors

No visitor journeys, session recordings, heatmaps or user profiles. SimpleDash reads totals and never stores anything about a single visitor.

## It doesn't replace GA4's deeper reports

Funnels, explorations, audiences, custom dimensions and attribution models stay in GA4. SimpleDash covers the questions most small sites ask every week: how many came, from where, what they read, and did anyone sign up or pay.

## It can't change your GA4

Access is read-only. SimpleDash can't create events, mark key events, change settings or add users. When something needs changing in GA4, our [guides](/guides) show you how to do it there.

## It can't show data GA4 doesn't have

- No visits from before you installed GA4.
- No visitors who refused cookies or block Google Analytics.
- No signups or sales GA4 isn't told about.

## Other things it doesn't do (yet)

- Google Ads data.
- Shared team accounts (share a [public page](/docs/public-page) instead).
- Languages other than English.
