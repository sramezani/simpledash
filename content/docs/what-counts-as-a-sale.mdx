With Pro, the **What brings customers** card shows where the people behind your signups and sales first found you, next to the visit it happened on. For sales, tell SimpleDash what counts as one: a GA4 key event like `purchase`, or views of a thank-you page.

## Choose it

Open the site's settings (the gear in the site menu) and find **What counts as a sale?**

- **Nothing yet**: the card shows signups only.
- **A key event**: pick one of your GA4 key events, usually `purchase`.
- **Views of a page**: type the path of the page people see after paying, such as `/thank-you`.

Then choose **Save**.

## Which one to pick

- If your checkout sends GA4's `purchase` event, pick it. It's the most accurate.
- If it doesn't, and buyers land on a thank-you page afterwards, use that page. [Count sales from a thank-you page](/guides/thank-you-page-conversions).
- If neither exists yet, [track purchases in GA4](/guides/track-purchases-ga4) first.

## The card

A **Signups | Sales** switch, then **By source** (First and Last visit), **Paths** (first visit → last visit) and **Landing pages**. Revenue (in **More**) appears only when GA4 itself has purchase values for the period (GA4's own sums, in your property's currency). Nothing is estimated. [What brings customers](/docs/what-brings-customers) explains each part.

## Sales going to Stripe or PayPal?

If your sales show up under `checkout.stripe.com` or another payment page, GA4 is crediting the payment page instead of where the buyer came from. [Fix it in two minutes](/guides/ga4-unwanted-referrals-stripe).
