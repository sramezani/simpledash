SimpleDash stores as little as possible, and never data about individual visitors: no IP addresses, no visitor IDs, no single visits. Google tokens and Stripe keys are encrypted, access to Google is read-only, and the code is open for anyone to check.

## What we store

| What | Details | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your account | Name, email and picture from Google; sign-in sessions (no IP addresses) | Until you delete the account; sessions until they expire |
| Google access | Access and refresh tokens, **encrypted** | Until you disconnect Google or delete the account |
| Your sites | Name, domain, time zone, your signup and sale choices, the Search Console property | Until you remove the site or delete the account |
| Report copies | Copies of report **totals** from Google (visitors per day, top pages, sources, countries, top searches with Search Console) so pages open fast | 7 days without use; never in backups |
| Your choices | Chart notes, the Monday email switch, public page settings | Until you delete them |
| Billing | Plan, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, renewal date. Card details stay with Stripe | Until you delete the account |
| Stripe totals (Pro, if connected) | Your read-only key, **encrypted**, and daily totals: charged, refunded, new customers, MRR. No customer names, emails or IDs | Until you remove Stripe |

## What we never store

- Anything about individual visitors: IP addresses, visitor IDs, single visits or journeys.
- Your card details (Stripe holds them).
- Customer names, emails or IDs from Stripe.

## How it's protected

- **Read-only access.** SimpleDash asks Google only for <Scope name="analytics" />, and <Scope name="search-console" /> if you connect Search Console. Stripe keys must be restricted read-only keys.
- **Encryption.** Google tokens and Stripe keys are encrypted before they're stored.
- **Checks on every request.** Every signed-in page and action checks on the server that the site belongs to you. Your site's data is never shared with another account.
- **Backups.** The database is backed up every night, without report copies and sign-in sessions, and backups are deleted after 30 days.
- **Open source.** You can read exactly how it works in <SourceRepo />.

## Our own analytics

simpledash.app uses Google Analytics too, only after you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner. The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains what's counted.

## Report a security problem

Email <SupportEmail /> with "Security" in the subject: what the problem is, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it. Please don't open a public issue. We'll reply quickly and credit you if you'd like.
