With Pro, SimpleDash emails you last week's numbers every Monday afternoon: one short block per site, Monday to Sunday, compared with the week before. It says what changed and never guesses why.

## What's in it

For each site:

- A [one-line summary](/docs/summary-line), like "Visitors up 23% vs the week before, mostly from Google."
- Visitors, Page views, Avg. time and Signups (if you've [chosen a signup action](/docs/signups)).
- The top 3 sources and the top 3 pages.
- A line about visitors from AI assistants, when there were any.
- **What moved**: up to three sources whose visitors changed most, with their main landing page, like "Google +300 visitors (120 → 420), mostly to /blog/ga4-setup." [What changed](/docs/what-changed).
- One line from [What brings customers](/docs/what-brings-customers) for the last 30 days, when one stands out.
- **This week on Google search**: the page to work on and its main fact, with a link to the steps, when Search Console is connected.
- A link to that site's dashboard.

The subject line gives your main site's headline number, for example "mysite.com: 1,234 visitors last week (up 23%)".

## When it arrives

On Monday, between 13:00 and 18:00 in your main site's time zone (the site you opened last). The afternoon start gives Google time to finish adding Sunday's visits.

Sites with no visitors in either week are left out, and if none had visitors, no email is sent.

## Turn it on or off

It's on by default for Pro. To turn it off, go to Settings → **Monday email**, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email (one click, no sign-in).

## Not getting it?

- Check that you're on [Pro](/docs/plans): the Monday email isn't part of Free.
- Look in your spam or promotions folder, and add <SupportEmail /> to your contacts.
- Make sure the email is on in Settings → Monday email.
