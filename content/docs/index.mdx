SimpleDash turns the Google Analytics 4 data a site already has into one simple dashboard page, a weekly Monday email and an optional public stats page. There is no tracking script: it reads GA4 with read-only access.

These docs explain every part of it in plain words. If you're new, start with [Get started](/docs/getting-started).

## How SimpleDash works

<Steps>
<Step>

**Sign in with Google.** You allow read-only access to Google Analytics. SimpleDash can't change anything in your account.

</Step>
<Step>

**We find your site.** If your Google account has several GA4 properties, SimpleDash opens the busiest one. You can switch to any other in one click.

</Step>
<Step>

**See everything on one page.** Visitors, where they came from, top pages, countries, devices, visitors from AI assistants, and who is on your site right now.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What SimpleDash is not

- **Not a tracking script.** SimpleDash adds nothing to your site. Every number comes from your own GA4 property, including the history GA4 already has.
- **Not a GA4 replacement.** GA4 keeps collecting the data. SimpleDash is a simpler way to read it.
- **Not for per-visitor tracking.** There are no visitor journeys, session recordings or heatmaps, and SimpleDash never stores data about individual visitors.

More on this in [What SimpleDash can't do](/docs/what-simpledash-cant-do).
