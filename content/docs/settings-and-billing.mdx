Settings has a menu on the left: **Profile**, **Plan & billing** and **Monday email** for your account, then one page per site. Billing goes through Stripe: upgrade, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card or cancel in a few clicks.

## Open Settings

Choose **Settings** at the top right of the dashboard. To open one site's settings straight away, use the gear next to it in the site menu.

## What's where

| Page | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | See your name and email, sign out, disconnect Google, delete your account |
| **Plan & billing** | Upgrade to Pro, manage billing, see when your plan renews or ends |
| **Monday email** | Turn the [Monday email](/docs/monday-email) on or off (Pro) |
| **A site** | Its [public page](/docs/public-page), [Google Search Console](/docs/google-search), [revenue from Stripe](/docs/revenue), [what counts as a sale](/docs/what-counts-as-a-sale), and removing the site |

## Upgrade to Pro

Go to **Plan & billing**, choose **Monthly** (<Price plan="pro" interval="month" />) or **Yearly** (<Price plan="pro" interval="year" />, <MonthsFree /> months free), then **Upgrade to Pro**. Stripe's checkout page opens; after paying you're back on your dashboard with Pro active.

Prices include tax. Stripe is the merchant of record, so it handles sales tax and VAT, and SimpleDash never sees your card.

## Manage billing

Once you're on Pro, **Manage billing** opens Stripe's billing page, where you can:

- update your card
- download invoices
- switch between monthly and yearly (Stripe shows the exact amount before you confirm)
- cancel

## Cancel

Choose **Manage billing**, then cancel in Stripe. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to Free. Nothing is deleted. Plan & billing shows the date it ends, for example "Pro · <Price plan="pro" interval="month" /> a month · ends on 2 Nov 2026".

## If a payment fails

Plan & billing shows a note asking you to update your card. Pro keeps working while Stripe retries the payment. Update the card in **Manage billing** to keep it running.
