The **default channel group** is GA4's way of sorting visits into a few buckets, such as Organic Search, Direct, Referral and Email, using rules on each visit's source and medium. It gives you a quick overview where the full list of sources would be too long.

## The channels, in plain words

| GA4 channel      | Means                                | SimpleDash calls it |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Direct           | No referrer and no tags              | Direct              |
| Organic Search   | Unpaid search engine results         | Search              |
| Organic Social   | Unpaid links from social sites       | Social              |
| Referral         | Links on other sites                 | Other sites         |
| Email            | Links tagged with `utm_medium=email` | Email               |
| AI Assistant     | Visits from AI assistants            | AI assistants       |
| Organic Video    | Unpaid video sites                   | Video               |
| Organic Shopping | Unpaid shopping sites                | Shopping            |
| Paid Search      | Search ads                           | Search ads          |
| Paid Social      | Social ads                           | Social ads          |
| Display          | Display ads                          | Display ads         |
| Unassigned       | Didn't match any rule                | Unknown             |

## How the rules work

GA4 looks at the source, the medium and sometimes the campaign name. For example, a medium of `email` makes a visit Email, and a known social site with no paid medium makes it Organic Social. A visit with an unusual medium may match nothing and become [Unassigned](/guides/ga4-unassigned-traffic).

## Your own channel groups

You can't edit the default group, but you can create your own (Admin → Data display → Channel groups), for example to group all AI assistants together. [How](/guides/ga4-ai-traffic#ai-channel-group).

## In SimpleDash

The **Sources** card has a **Channels** tab with these channels in plain words.
