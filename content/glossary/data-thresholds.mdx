**Data thresholds** are GA4 hiding rows or numbers in a report when they're so small that someone might be identified, for example when demographics or Google signals are involved. You'll see an orange triangle or a warning that data was withheld. It mostly affects small sites and narrow date ranges.

## When thresholds apply

- **Google signals** is on, and the report uses data from it.
- The report includes **demographics** (age, gender, interests).
- The numbers are **small**: a few users for a row.

## How to tell

Look for an orange triangle or a "thresholds applied" notice next to the report's title. Some rows may be missing, and totals may not add up.

## How to avoid them

- **Reporting identity:** Admin → Data display → **Reporting identity**. Choosing **Device-based** usually removes thresholds, because it doesn't use Google signals.
- **Wider date ranges** make rows bigger than the threshold.
- **Fewer dimensions** in a report mean bigger rows.

Changing reporting identity only changes how reports are shown, not the data collected, and you can switch back.

## In SimpleDash

SimpleDash doesn't ask GA4 for demographics, so thresholds rarely affect it. If a list looks shorter than expected, a wider date range usually helps.
