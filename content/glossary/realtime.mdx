GA4's **Realtime** report shows what's happening on your site in the last **30 minutes**: active users, pages, events and where people came from. It's perfect for checking that tracking works or watching a launch, but its numbers don't match the standard reports, which take hours to process.

## What it shows

Reports → **Realtime overview**:

- Active users in the last 30 minutes (and the last 5)
- Users by source, page or screen, country
- Events and key events as they happen

## What it's good for

- **Checking tracking.** Open your site and see yourself appear. [How](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).
- **Watching a launch.** See the spike from a post or newsletter as it happens.
- **Testing events.** Sign up on your own site and watch `sign_up` arrive.

## Why it doesn't match other reports

Realtime counts the last 30 minutes as they come in. Standard reports are processed first (usually hours later) and can include visits that arrived late. Don't add up Realtime numbers to get a day's total.

## In SimpleDash

The **Right now** card shows the same last-30-minutes data on a small globe, with the pages people are on (by page title, because that's what Google's realtime data has). It updates about every 45 seconds while the page is open, and you can add it to your public stats page.
