**Attribution** is how Google Analytics decides which source gets the credit for a signup or a sale, when the person visited several times from different places before converting. GA4 uses **data-driven** attribution by default for key events, and you can switch to **last click**.

## An example

Someone finds your blog on Google, comes back from your newsletter a week later, and finally types your address and buys. Which source "made" the sale?

- **Last click** gives it all to the last non-direct source: the newsletter.
- **Data-driven** shares it between Google and the newsletter, based on patterns GA4 sees across your conversions.

## Which models GA4 has

Admin → Data display → **Attribution settings**:

- **Data-driven** (the default): credit is shared using your own data.
- **Paid and organic last click**: all credit to the last non-direct click.
- **Google paid channels last click**: for Google Ads users.

Older models like first click and linear were removed in 2023.

## Which reports use it

- **Advertising** reports and key event credit by source use your attribution model.
- **Traffic acquisition** shows each session's own source, so a conversion appears under the source of the visit where it happened.

That's why the same sale can show under different sources in different reports.

## For small sites

With few conversions, data-driven attribution has little to learn from, and the session view is easier to read: "this visit, from this source, ended in a signup". Read sources as a guide, not an exact split of credit.

## In SimpleDash

With Pro, **Sales and signups by source** uses each visit's own source and landing page, so it reads like GA4's Traffic acquisition report: no model to configure.
