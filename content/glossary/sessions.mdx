A **session** in Google Analytics 4 is one visit: a period when someone is using your site. It starts when they arrive and ends after 30 minutes without activity (by default). One person can have many sessions, so you'll always have more sessions than users.

## When a session starts and ends

- **Starts:** when someone opens your site and GA4 records `session_start`.
- **Ends:** after 30 minutes of no activity. You can change this timeout in the data stream's tag settings (Adjust session timeout).

Unlike the old Universal Analytics, GA4 **doesn't** start a new session at midnight or when the traffic source changes mid-visit.

## Sessions vs users

|              | Counts                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| **Users**    | People (really, browsers) |
| **Sessions** | Visits                    |

If 100 users visit twice each, that's 100 users and 200 sessions.

## Session source

Each session has one source and medium: where that visit came from. Traffic acquisition reports use session-level sources. [Source and medium](/glossary/source-medium).

## In SimpleDash

SimpleDash leads with **Visitors** (active users) rather than sessions, because "how many people came" is the clearer question. Sources are by session, like GA4's Traffic acquisition report.
