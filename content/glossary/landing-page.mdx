A **landing page** is the first page someone saw in a visit: your homepage, a blog post, a docs page. Landing pages show what brings people in, which is often different from the pages they view most.

## Why it matters

- **For SEO:** landing pages from Google show which pages rank and bring visitors.
- **For launches:** you see whether people arrive on the page you shared.
- **For signups:** with key events, you see which first pages lead to signups.

## Where to find it

Reports → Engagement → **Landing page**, with sessions, users and key events for each one. You can also add **Landing page** as a dimension in Traffic acquisition to see which source brought people to which page.

## Landing page shows (not set)

When a session has no page view (for example, it started with another event, or the tag fired late), GA4 has no landing page for it and shows **(not set)**. A small share is normal. [(not set) explained](/glossary/not-set).

## In SimpleDash

The **Pages** card has a **Landing pages** tab: where visits started, by visitors. With Pro, **Sales and signups by source** has a **By landing page** view.
