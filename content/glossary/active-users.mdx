**Active users** is the main visitor number in Google Analytics 4: the number of distinct people who were engaged on your site in a period. When GA4 says "Users" in most reports, it means active users. SimpleDash calls it **Visitors**.

## Active users vs total users vs new users

| GA4 metric       | Counts                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active users** | People who had an engaged session, visited for the first time, or spent time with the site in view |
| **Total users**  | Everyone who triggered any event, engaged or not                                                   |
| **New users**    | People visiting for the first time in the period                                                   |

Active users is usually close to total users, and slightly lower. GA4's reports use active users by default.

## How GA4 recognises a person

GA4 recognises a returning visitor by a cookie in their browser. So the same person on their phone and laptop counts as two users, someone who clears cookies counts as new, and people who block GA4 or decline cookies aren't counted. "Users" are really browsers.

## Where to find it

Reports → **Acquisition overview** (the Active users card), and the "Active users" column in most reports.

## In SimpleDash

**Visitors** is GA4's active users, for the same dates, with the help text "Visitors that Google Analytics counted. People who refuse cookies or block GA aren't included."
