Google Analytics 4 doesn't show visits in its standard reports straight away: it processes them first, which usually takes several hours and can take a day or more. That's why today's numbers start low, why yesterday can still change, and why only the Realtime report is truly live.

## How long the delay is

Google's own figures for standard (free) properties, from its [data freshness](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161) page:

- **Realtime report:** within minutes, covering the last 30 minutes.
- **Intraday data:** usually 2 to 6 hours.
- **Daily data:** about 12 hours, and processing "can take 24–48 hours".

So a day's numbers keep growing for a while after the day ends. Comparing "today so far" with a full day will always look like a drop.

## What this means for you

- **Check launches in Realtime**, not in the standard reports. [How](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).
- **Wait before judging a day.** Look at yesterday's numbers in the afternoon, not first thing in the morning.
- **Compare like with like.** Today so far against a whole day isn't a fair comparison.

## Other reasons numbers look low

- **Consent.** Visitors who decline cookies aren't counted the same way. [Cookie banners and GA4](/guides/ga4-cookie-consent).
- **Ad blockers** stop GA4 for some visitors, more on tech-savvy audiences.
- **Thresholds.** GA4 can hide small numbers in some reports to protect privacy. [Data thresholds](/glossary/data-thresholds).
