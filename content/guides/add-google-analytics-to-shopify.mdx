The recommended way to add Google Analytics 4 to Shopify is the free **Google & YouTube** app from Shopify: install it, connect your Google account, and choose your GA4 property. It adds the tag and sends store events like add-to-cart and purchases, so you don't need to edit your theme.

## Connect GA4

<Steps>
<Step>

In Shopify admin, install the **Google & YouTube** app from the Shopify App Store.

</Step>
<Step>

Open the app and connect the Google account that owns your GA4 property.

</Step>
<Step>

In the app's settings, connect **Google Analytics** and choose your GA4 property (or create one).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Don't add the tag twice

If your theme or an older app already has a GA4 tag, remove it after connecting the app, or visits and purchases are counted twice. Check by searching your theme code (Online Store → Themes → Edit code) for `gtag`.

## Purchases

The app sends GA4's ecommerce events, including `purchase` with the order value, so GA4 shows revenue by source. Mark `purchase` as a key event if it isn't already. [Key events](/guides/ga4-key-events).

## Check it works

Visit your store in a private window and look at GA4's **Realtime overview**. A test order shows up as a purchase event. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

<InSimpleDash>
  With Pro, pick `purchase` as what counts as a sale, and SimpleDash's **Sales and signups by
  source** shows which sources and landing pages bring orders, with GA4's own revenue.
</InSimpleDash>
