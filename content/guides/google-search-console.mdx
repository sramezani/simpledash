Google Search Console shows how your site does in Google search: which searches show your pages, how often people click, and your average position. For a small site, four habits do most of the work: find searches close to page 1, fix pages people see but don't click, watch what changed, and keep an eye on searches for your name.

## The four numbers

| Search Console       | In plain words                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clicks**           | Visits from Google search                                                 |
| **Impressions**      | How often your pages appeared in results (SimpleDash calls this **Seen**) |
| **CTR**              | Click rate: clicks ÷ impressions                                          |
| **Average position** | Where your page appeared, on average (1 is the top)                       |

Find them in Search Console → **Performance → Search results**. Tick all four boxes at the top to see them together.

## Set up first

If your site isn't in Search Console yet, [add and verify it](/guides/add-site-to-search-console), then submit your sitemap (Indexing → Sitemaps).

## Four weekly habits

### 1. Find searches close to page 1

Searches where you rank around positions 8 to 15 are near the bottom of page 1 or top of page 2. A better title, a clearer section or a few internal links can move them up. [How](/guides/keywords-close-to-page-1).

### 2. Fix pages seen but not clicked

A page that appears a lot but rarely gets clicked usually needs a better title and description. [How](/guides/low-click-rate-pages).

### 3. Check what changed

When clicks go up or down, find the pages and searches behind it, and look at position and impressions for each. [How](/guides/google-clicks-what-changed).

### 4. Watch your name vs new people

Searches for your brand show people who already know you; everything else is new people finding you. Growth in the second is what SEO is for. [How](/guides/branded-vs-non-branded-traffic).

## Other reports worth a look

- **Indexing → Pages**: which pages Google hasn't indexed, and why.
- **Experience → Core Web Vitals**: pages that load slowly for real visitors.
- **Links**: who links to you.

## Good to know

- **Data is 2 to 3 days behind**, and Search Console keeps 16 months.
- **Rare searches are hidden.** Google leaves out searches made by very few people, so per-search lists add up to less than your total clicks.
- **Clicks aren't GA4 visits.** [Why the numbers differ](/guides/ga4-numbers-differ).
