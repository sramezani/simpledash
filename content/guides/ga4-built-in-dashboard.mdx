Google Analytics 4 now has a built-in dashboard: one page of cards you build yourself, inside GA4. To make one, open **Reports**, click **+ Create** at the top of the left menu, choose **Dashboard**, drag charts onto the page, then **Save** and **Publish**.

You need the Editor or Administrator role to build one. Anyone with access to the property can view it once it's published.

## What it can show

| Card        | Shows                                                |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Score card  | One number, with the % change when you compare dates |
| Table       | Rows like pages or sources, with shaded bars         |
| Line chart  | A metric over time, by day, week or month            |
| Bar chart   | Rows compared side by side                           |
| Donut chart | How parts make up a whole, like devices              |
| Funnel      | Steps towards a goal, and where people drop off      |

## Create a dashboard

<Steps>
<Step>

In GA4, open **Reports** in the left menu.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **+ Create** at the top of the reports menu, then **Dashboard**.

</Step>
<Step>

Drag a card type, such as a score card, onto the grid.

</Step>
<Step>

Drag the metrics and dimensions you want onto the card. Change its details in the panel on the right.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Save**, then **Publish** to add the dashboard to the reports menu, where everyone with access to the property can open it.

</Step>
</Steps>

To change it later, open it from the reports menu and click the edit icon.

## Six cards for a small site

These answer the questions that matter ([which ones and why](/guides/understand-website-traffic)), using GA4's names:

1. **Score card: Active users.** Your visitors.
2. **Score card: Key events.** Signups or sales, once you've [marked them](/guides/ga4-key-events).
3. **Line chart: Active users by date.** The trend.
4. **Table: Session source, with Active users.** Where people came from.
5. **Table: Page path, with Views.** Your top pages.
6. **Table: Landing page, with Sessions.** Where visits start.

Then turn on **Compare** in the date menu, so the score cards show the change from the period before.

## Its limits

- **Up to 15 cards** on a standard property (30 on Google Analytics 360).
- **No segments and no comparisons inside a single card.**
- **No API**, so other tools can't read it.
- **Shared with the whole property** once published.
- **One property at a time.** For several sites, see [all your GA4 sites in one dashboard](/guides/ga4-multiple-properties-one-dashboard).
- **GA4's words.** It shows "active users" and "key events", so it's easiest if you already know them ([plain words](/glossary)).
