The quickest check: open your site in one tab and GA4's **Reports → Realtime overview** in another. If you appear within a minute or two, GA4 is collecting data. If you don't, the cause is almost always an ad blocker, a cookie banner, a wrong Measurement ID or a tag that isn't on the page.

## 1. Realtime

<Steps>
<Step>

In [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com), open **Reports → Realtime overview**.

</Step>
<Step>

In another tab (ideally a private window with no ad blocker), open your site and click to a second page.

</Step>
<Step>

Within a minute or two you should see at least one user in the last 30 minutes, and your pages in the list.

</Step>
</Steps>

## 2. DebugView, for events

DebugView shows each event as it arrives, which helps when you're setting up signups or purchases.

1. Install Google's [Tag Assistant](https://tagassistant.google.com) or open your site through it: enter your site's address and choose **Connect**.
2. In GA4, open Admin → Data display → **DebugView**.
3. Use your site: page views, clicks and your own events appear in a timeline within seconds.

## 3. Your browser's network tab

1. Open your site, then your browser's developer tools (**F12**, or **⌥⌘I** on a Mac) → **Network**.
2. Type `collect` in the filter box and reload the page.
3. You should see requests to `google-analytics.com/g/collect` with your `G-…` ID in them. If there are none, the tag isn't running.

## Not showing up? The usual reasons

| Reason                                 | How to check                                                    | Fix                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An ad blocker or privacy browser       | Try a private window with extensions off, or another browser    | Nothing to fix: those visitors aren't counted, by design                            |
| The cookie banner                      | Did you accept cookies on your own site?                        | Accept, then reload. With Consent Mode, declined visits aren't counted the same way |
| A wrong Measurement ID                 | Compare the `G-…` in your page source with Admin → Data streams | Paste the right ID                                                                  |
| The tag isn't on every page            | View the page source (**Ctrl/⌘ U**) and search for `gtag`       | Put the tag in the shared layout or header                                          |
| Your internal traffic filter is active | Admin → Data filters → is "Internal Traffic" **Active**?        | Expected: your own visits are excluded on purpose                                   |

If you just added GA4, give standard reports a few hours: only Realtime is immediate. [Why GA4 data is delayed](/guides/ga4-data-delay).
