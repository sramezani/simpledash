With Google Tag Manager, add GA4 as a **Google tag**: create a new tag of that type, enter your Measurement ID (`G-…`), set it to fire on **Initialization – All Pages**, then preview and **Submit** to publish. Don't also paste the gtag code on your site, or visits are counted twice.

## Before you start

You need a GTM container installed on your site (the two snippets from tagmanager.google.com, one in `<head>`, one after `<body>`), and your GA4 Measurement ID. [Create GA4](/guides/add-google-analytics-to-website).

## Add the Google tag

<Steps>
<Step>

In [Tag Manager](https://tagmanager.google.com), open your container and choose **Tags → New**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Tag configuration → Google Tag**, and enter your Measurement ID as the **Tag ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Triggering → Initialization – All Pages**.

</Step>
<Step>

Name it "GA4 – Google tag" and **Save**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Preview**, open your site in the Tag Assistant window and check the tag fires. Then choose **Submit → Publish**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Send a signup event

When signup succeeds, push an event to the data layer from your site:

```js
window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
window.dataLayer.push({ event: "sign_up", method: "Email" });
```

In GTM, create a **Custom Event** trigger with event name `sign_up`, and a **Google Analytics: GA4 Event** tag with event name `sign_up` that fires on it. Publish, then [mark `sign_up` as a key event](/guides/ga4-key-events).

## Check it works

After publishing, open your site and GA4's **Realtime overview**. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

<InSimpleDash>
  However you install GA4, SimpleDash reads the result. Pick `sign_up` as your signup action and it
  shows signups next to visitors, compared with the period before.
</InSimpleDash>
