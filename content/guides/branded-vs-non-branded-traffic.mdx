Branded searches contain your site's name ("simpledash login"); non-branded ones don't ("simple ga4 dashboard"). Branded clicks show people who already know you; non-branded clicks show new people finding you, which is what SEO is meant to grow. In Search Console, split them with a **query filter** that uses a regex of your name.

## Split them in Search Console

<Steps>
<Step>

Open Search Console → **Performance → Search results**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **+ Add filter → Query → Custom (regex)**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Matches regex** and enter your name and its common spellings, for example:

```text
simple ?dash|simpledash\.app
```

That's your branded traffic.

</Step>
<Step>

Edit the filter to **Doesn't match regex** with the same pattern: that's your non-branded traffic.

</Step>
</Steps>

Add misspellings and your product names to the pattern. `?` makes the space optional, and `|` means "or".

## Why the two don't add up to your total

Google leaves out searches made by very few people ("anonymized queries") from any query-filtered view. So branded plus non-branded is less than your total clicks, sometimes much less on small sites. Compare each one with itself over time rather than adding them up.

## What to look for

- **Non-branded growing**: your content is reaching new people.
- **Branded growing**: more people know your name, often after a launch or press.
- **Branded falling** while non-branded holds: fewer people looking for you directly.
