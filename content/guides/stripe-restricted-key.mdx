A restricted key is a Stripe API key with only the permissions you choose. To share revenue numbers safely with an app like SimpleDash, create a key with custom permissions: **Read** access to Charges, Refunds, Customers and Subscriptions, and **None** for everything else. It can't charge cards, refund payments or change your account.

## Why a restricted key, not your secret key

Your **secret key** (`sk_live_…`) can do anything in your Stripe account, including moving money. Never paste it into another service. A **restricted key** (`rk_live_…`) can do only what you allow, and you can delete it at any time without affecting anything else.

## Create the key

<Steps>
<Step>

Sign in to the [Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com) and open **Developers** → **API keys**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Create secret key**. On **Assign permissions to your key**, choose **custom permissions**. Don't choose full access, which creates an unrestricted secret key.

</Step>
<Step>

In **Key name**, name it after the app you'll give it to, for example "SimpleDash", so you'll know later what it's for.

</Step>
<Step>

Set these four to **Read**:

- **Charges**
- **Refunds**
- **Customers**
- **Subscriptions**

Leave every other permission on **None**.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Create key** and complete Stripe's two-factor check. Then click the key to copy it. It starts with `rk_live_` (or `rk_test_` in a sandbox or test mode). Copy it now: Stripe won't show it again.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Keep it safe

- Paste it only into the app it's for. Don't share it in chats or email.
- To stop the app's access, delete the key in Developers → API keys. Nothing else is affected.
- If you think it leaked, delete it and create a new one.

## Try it in a sandbox first?

Create the key in a Stripe **sandbox** (or test mode) to try things out with test payments. Those keys start with `rk_test_`.

<InSimpleDash>
  With Pro, paste the key under **Revenue from Stripe** in your site's settings and choose **Check
  and save**. SimpleDash checks the four permissions, stores the key encrypted, and shows revenue,
  MRR and new customers next to your visitors. It refuses secret keys on purpose. [Revenue from

</InSimpleDash>
