In Wix, go to **Marketing & SEO → Marketing Integrations → Google Analytics**, choose **Connect**, and paste your GA4 Measurement ID (`G-…`). Wix adds the tag to your whole site. It needs a premium plan with a connected domain.

## Connect GA4

<Steps>
<Step>

In your Wix dashboard, open **Marketing & SEO** → **Marketing Integrations**.

</Step>
<Step>

Find **Google Analytics** and choose **Connect**.

</Step>
<Step>

Paste your Measurement ID, which starts with `G-`, and save.

</Step>
</Steps>

  id="wix-google-analytics"

  In a Wix dashboard, Marketing & SEO → Marketing Integrations → Google Analytics. Capture the
  connect form with a placeholder G-XXXXXXXXXX ID.
</ImageSlot>

## Check it works

Open your published site in a private window and GA4's **Realtime overview**. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

<InSimpleDash>
  Once visits arrive, SimpleDash shows your Wix site's visitors, where they come from and what they
  read on one page, with a public stats page if you want to share it.
</InSimpleDash>
