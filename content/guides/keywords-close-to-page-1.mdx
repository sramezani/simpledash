Searches where your pages rank around positions **8 to 15** sit at the bottom of page 1 or the top of page 2, and are the cheapest wins in SEO. In Search Console, filter the Performance report to those positions, sort by impressions, and improve the page that ranks for each one.

## Find them in Search Console

<Steps>
<Step>

Open Search Console → **Performance → Search results**, and choose the last 3 months.

</Step>
<Step>

Tick **Average position** and **Impressions** at the top.

</Step>
<Step>

In the **Queries** table, use the filter icon to add **Position** is greater than `7.5`, and another filter for **Position** is smaller than `15.5`.

</Step>
<Step>

Sort by **Impressions**, highest first. Searches that many people make, where you're almost on page 1, are at the top.

</Step>
<Step>

Click a search, then the **Pages** tab, to see which page ranks for it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Improve each page

For each search, open the page that ranks and check:

1. **Does the page answer that search directly?** Add a short, clear answer near the top, or a section with the search as its heading.
2. **Is the search in the title?** Put the main words in the page's title and H1, naturally.
3. **Do other pages link to it?** Add links from related pages on your site, with words that describe it.
4. **Is it up to date?** Refresh old facts and screenshots.

Change one page at a time and give it two to four weeks: rankings move slowly.

## Pick the right searches

Skip searches that don't fit your page or your product, even with many impressions. A search that matches what you offer is worth more than a bigger one that doesn't.
