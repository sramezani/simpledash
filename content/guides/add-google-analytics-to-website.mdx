To add Google Analytics 4, create a GA4 account and property at analytics.google.com, add a web data stream for your site, then paste the Google tag (a short script with your Measurement ID, `G-…`) into the `<head>` of every page. It takes about five minutes, and visits start showing within a minute in GA4's Realtime report.

## What you need

- A Google account (the one you want to own the analytics).
- Access to your site's code, or to its builder's settings (Webflow, WordPress, Framer and so on).

If your site runs on a builder, the platform guides below are quicker: most have a field where you paste the Measurement ID.

## 1. Create a Google Analytics account and property

<Steps>
<Step>

Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com) and choose **Start measuring**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Account:** give it a name, usually your company or your own name. One account can hold many sites.

</Step>
<Step>

**Property:** name it after your site (for example "mysite.com"), and set the **time zone** and **currency** you work in. The time zone decides when your days start and end, in GA4 and in SimpleDash.

</Step>
<Step>

Answer the questions about your business and goals. They only change which reports GA4 suggests.

</Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Add a web data stream

<Steps>
<Step>

When GA4 asks where to collect data, choose **Web**.

</Step>
<Step>

Enter your site's address (for example `mysite.com`) and a stream name.

</Step>
<Step>

Leave **Enhanced measurement** on. It counts page views, scrolls, outbound clicks, file downloads, form interactions and site searches without extra code.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Create stream** (or **Create & continue**). GA4 shows your **Measurement ID**, which starts with `G-`.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can find the Measurement ID again any time in Admin → Data collection and modification → **Data streams** → your stream.

## 3. Add the Google tag to every page

GA4 shows installation instructions. Choose **Install manually** and copy the code, which looks like this (with your own ID instead of `G-XXXXXXXXXX`):

```html title="Paste right after <head>"
<!-- Google tag (gtag.js) -->
<script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"></script>
<script>
  window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
  function gtag() {
    dataLayer.push(arguments);
  }
  gtag("js", new Date());

  gtag("config", "G-XXXXXXXXXX");
</script>
```

Paste it right after the opening `<head>` tag on **every page** of your site. On most sites that means one shared layout or header file.

## 4. Check it works

Open your site in another tab, then open GA4's **Reports → Realtime overview**. You should appear within a minute. If you don't, see [Check that GA4 is collecting data](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

## Do you need a cookie banner?

If your visitors are in the EU, the UK or another place with similar rules, you usually need consent before GA4 sets its cookies. [Cookie banners and Consent Mode for GA4](/guides/ga4-cookie-consent) explains the options. This isn't legal advice.
