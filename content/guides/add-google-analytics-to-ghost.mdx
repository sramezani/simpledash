To add Google Analytics 4 to Ghost, copy the Google tag from GA4 and paste it into **Settings → Code injection → Site header** in Ghost Admin, then save. It's added to every page of your publication straight away.

## Add the tag

<Steps>
<Step>

In GA4, open Admin → Data streams → your stream → **View tag instructions** → **Install manually**, and copy the code.

</Step>
<Step>

In Ghost Admin, open **Settings** and find **Code injection** (under Advanced).

</Step>
<Step>

Paste the code into **Site header** and choose **Save**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Count newsletter signups

Ghost sends new members to a confirmation flow. A simple, code-free way to count signups is to create a `sign_up` event in GA4 from views of the page members land on after signing up. [Track signups without code](/guides/track-signups-ga4#no-code).

## Check it works

Open your site in a private window and GA4's **Realtime overview**. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

<InSimpleDash>
  SimpleDash shows which posts readers land on, where they come from (newsletters, Google, X, AI
  assistants) and how many sign up, on one page. A public stats page lets you share your
  publication's growth with readers.
</InSimpleDash>
