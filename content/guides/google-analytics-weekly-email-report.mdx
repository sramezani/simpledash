To get a weekly Google Analytics report by email, open the GA4 report you want, click **Share this report** at the top right, choose **Schedule email**, set it to **Weekly** and save. GA4 then emails that report as a PDF or CSV file on the day you picked. You need to be an Administrator of the property.

It's free and built in. What it doesn't do is tell you what changed: you get one report's table, not a summary.

## Before you start

- GA4 collecting data on your site ([check it](/guides/check-ga4-is-working)).
- The **Administrator** role on the GA4 property. Only administrators can schedule emails: if **Schedule email** is missing from the menu, that's why.
- Recipients must already have access to the property in GA4 (up to 50 people). To add someone, see [how to share Google Analytics](/guides/share-google-analytics).
- Pick the report first. For most small sites, **Traffic acquisition** (visitors by channel) or **Pages and screens** (views per page) is the one to send. [Which numbers matter](/guides/understand-website-traffic).

## Schedule the email

<Steps>
<Step>

In GA4, open **Reports** and the report you want to receive, with the dates and comparison you want.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Share this report** (the share icon at the top right), then **Schedule email**.

</Step>
<Step>

Give the email a name (it becomes the subject: "GA4 Scheduled Email: your name"), choose the recipients from the people with access, choose **Weekly** and the day, and pick **PDF** or **CSV**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Save**. The first email arrives on the next day you picked, with the dates, filters and comparison the report had when you scheduled it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Change or stop a scheduled email

In **Admin**, open the **Scheduled reports** page for the property. Each email shows its status and can be edited or deleted there. Recipients can also stop it with the **Unsubscribe** link in the email.

## What you get, and its limits

| What                       | GA4's scheduled email                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| How often                  | Daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly               |
| What's in it               | One report, as a PDF or CSV file                  |
| Who can receive it         | Only people with access to the property, up to 50 |
| Realtime                   | Can't be scheduled                                |
| Several sites in one email | No: one property per email                        |
| What changed and why       | No summary: you compare the numbers yourself      |
| Words                      | GA4's own (active users, sessions, key events)    |
| Cost                       | Free                                              |
