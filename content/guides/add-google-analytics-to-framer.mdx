To add Google Analytics 4 to Framer, copy your Measurement ID (it starts with `G-`) from GA4, paste it into **Site Settings → Google Analytics Measurement ID** in your Framer project, save and publish. No code needed.

## Before you start

Create a GA4 property and web stream, and copy its Measurement ID. [How](/guides/add-google-analytics-to-website). You'll find it again in GA4 under Admin → Data streams → your stream.

## Add your Measurement ID

<Steps>
<Step>

In Framer, open your project's **Site Settings**.

</Step>
<Step>

Paste your Measurement ID into **Google Analytics Measurement ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

Save, then **Publish** your site. The tag is added to the published site, not to Framer's editor.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Need the full Google tag?

If you want to add Consent Mode or other settings, paste the full Google tag into **Custom Code** (in the head section) instead, and leave the Measurement ID field empty, so the tag isn't loaded twice.

## Page changes

Framer sites change pages without a full reload. GA4's **enhanced measurement** counts those page changes automatically, as long as _Page changes based on browser history events_ is on (Admin → Data streams → your stream → Enhanced measurement → Page views).

## Check it works

Open your published site and GA4's **Realtime overview**. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).

<InSimpleDash>
  Once visits arrive, sign in to SimpleDash with the same Google account to see your Framer site's
  visitors, sources and top pages on one page, plus a public stats page you can share in one click.
</InSimpleDash>
