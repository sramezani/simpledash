Carrd connects Google Analytics through its **Site Analytics** setting: in your Carrd dashboard, open the site's **Manage** page, choose **Analytics**, select **Google Analytics** as the provider, add your GA4 Measurement ID (`G-…`) and enable it. It needs Carrd Pro Standard or higher.

## Add Google Analytics

<Steps>
<Step>

Open your Carrd **Dashboard** and the **Sites** tab.

</Step>
<Step>

Find your site and click its **Manage** icon.

</Step>
<Step>

In the sidebar, click **Analytics**. Under **Site Analytics**, click **Select Provider** and choose **Google Analytics**.

</Step>
<Step>

Enter your Measurement ID, which starts with `G-`, and click **Enable**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Count signups from a Carrd form

Carrd elements have **Events** (select an element → Settings → Events), which can run code such as a `gtag('event', 'sign_up')` call when a form is submitted. Then [mark `sign_up` as a key event](/guides/ga4-key-events).

## Check it works

Open your published page in a private window and GA4's **Realtime overview**. [More ways to check](/guides/check-ga4-is-working).
