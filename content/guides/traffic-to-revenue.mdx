To know which sources bring paying customers, GA4 has to see the sale: send a `purchase` event (or count a thank-you page), stop payment pages taking the credit, then read purchases by session source and landing page. Stripe tells you how much money came in; GA4 tells you where buyers came from.

## The setup, in order

<Steps>
<Step>

**Send the sale to GA4.** A [purchase event](/guides/track-purchases-ga4) with value and currency, or, without code, views of your [thank-you page](/guides/thank-you-page-conversions).

</Step>
<Step>

**Mark it as a key event.** [How](/guides/ga4-key-events).

</Step>
<Step>

**Stop payment pages taking the credit.** Add Stripe, PayPal and other payment pages to [unwanted referrals](/guides/ga4-unwanted-referrals-stripe). Otherwise many sales show as coming from `checkout.stripe.com`.

</Step>
<Step>

**Tag the links you control.** Newsletters, posts and ads with [UTM links](/guides/utm-links).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Read it in GA4

Reports → Acquisition → **Traffic acquisition**, then choose your purchase key event in the **Key events** column (the dropdown under its heading). Switch the first column between **Session source / medium** and **Session default channel group**. For pages, use Reports → Engagement → **Landing page**.

## What it can and can't tell you

- **It shows the source of the visit where the sale happened.** If someone read your blog from Google, came back a week later by typing your address and bought, GA4's session view credits Direct.
- **Not every buyer is tracked.** Buyers who block GA4 still pay. Stripe's total will be higher than GA4's.
- **Revenue in GA4 comes from your event**, not from Stripe. If they disagree, Stripe is right about money.

So use GA4 to compare sources with each other, and Stripe for your real revenue.

## Avoid made-up numbers

It's tempting to divide revenue by visitors per source to get "revenue per visitor from X". Unless GA4 actually recorded the sale for that source, that number is a guess. Keep to sales GA4 recorded, and revenue Stripe recorded.
