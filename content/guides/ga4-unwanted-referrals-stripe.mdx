When a buyer goes from your site to Stripe Checkout and comes back, GA4 can start a new visit with `checkout.stripe.com` as its source, so the sale is credited to Stripe instead of Google, X or wherever the buyer came from. The fix takes two minutes: add the payment page to GA4's **List unwanted referrals**.

## Do you have this problem?

In GA4, open Reports → Acquisition → **Traffic acquisition** and look for these sources:

- `checkout.stripe.com`
- `paypal.com`, `www.paypal.com`
- `checkout.paddle.com`, Paddle
- `lemonsqueezy.com` or your store's `*.lemonsqueezy.com` address
- `polar.sh`

If any of them brings visitors or key events, your sales are being misattributed.

## Fix it

<Steps>
<Step>

In GA4, open Admin → Data collection and modification → **Data streams**, and choose your web stream.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Google tag**, choose **Configure tag settings**, then **Show more** (or **Show all**).

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **List unwanted referrals**.

</Step>
<Step>

Add a condition: match type **Referral domain contains**, domain `checkout.stripe.com`. Add a row for each payment provider you use, for example `paypal.com`.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Save**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What changes

From now on, a visit that returns from the payment page keeps its original source, so the purchase is credited to where the buyer really came from. It only affects new visits: past data stays as it was.

## Don't add your own domain

GA4 already ignores moves within your own site, and between the domains you've set up for cross-domain measurement. Adding your own domain as an unwanted referral can hide real tagging problems. If your shop or app lives on another domain of yours, set up [cross-domain measurement](/guides/ga4-cross-domain) instead.

<InSimpleDash>
  SimpleDash's **Sources** card warns you when a payment page like `checkout.stripe.com`, PayPal,
  Paddle, Lemon Squeezy or Polar shows up as a source, and links back to this fix. The warning is
  only on your dashboard, never on public pages.
</InSimpleDash>
