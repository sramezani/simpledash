UTM tags are labels you add to the end of a link, like `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch`, so Google Analytics knows exactly where a visit came from. Use them on links you share in newsletters, social posts and communities; without them, many of those visits show up as "Direct" or as a vague referrer.

## The five tags

| Tag            | What it says                     | Examples                                   |
| -------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `utm_source`   | **Who** sent the visit           | `x`, `producthunt`, `newsletter`, `reddit` |
| `utm_medium`   | **What kind** of link            | `social`, `email`, `referral`, `cpc`       |
| `utm_campaign` | **Which** campaign               | `launch`, `black-friday`, `oct-update`     |
| `utm_content`  | Which link or version (optional) | `header-link`, `video-post`                |
| `utm_term`     | A paid keyword (optional)        | `ga4-dashboard`                            |

`utm_source` is required for GA4 to use the others. Use lowercase and dashes, and stick to the same words every time: GA4 treats `X` and `x` as two sources.

## Example: a launch week

```text
Product Hunt:  https://mysite.com/?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=launch
X post:        https://mysite.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch
Newsletter:    https://mysite.com/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch
```

## Pick the medium carefully

GA4 sorts visits into channels (Search, Social, Email and so on) using the medium. Use the common values, `social`, `email`, `referral`, `cpc`, `affiliate`, or the visit can end up as **Unassigned**. [Why traffic is Unassigned](/guides/ga4-unassigned-traffic).

## Build links without typos

Google's free [Campaign URL Builder](https://ga-dev-tools.google/campaign-url-builder/) fills in the tags for you. Keep a small spreadsheet of the links you've used so your naming stays the same.

## When not to use UTM tags

**Never on links within your own site.** A UTM tag starts a new visit with that source, which breaks the original source of the visitor. Use them only on links that point to your site from somewhere else.

## Where to see the results

In GA4: Reports → Acquisition → **Traffic acquisition**, and change the table's first column to **Session source / medium** or **Session campaign**.
