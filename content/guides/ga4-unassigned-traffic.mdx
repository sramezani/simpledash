**Unassigned** in Google Analytics 4 means a visit had information about where it came from, but it didn't match any of GA4's channel rules. Most often the link's `utm_medium` was an unusual word GA4 doesn't recognise. Fix your UTM tags and new visits land in the right channel.

## Find what's unassigned

<Steps>
<Step>

Open Reports → Acquisition → **Traffic acquisition**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Unassigned** in the table to filter to it, or add a filter on **Session default channel group** = Unassigned.

</Step>
<Step>

Change the first column to **Session source / medium**. The values tell you what went wrong.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Common causes and fixes

| You see                                   | Why                                                   | Fix                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `newsletter / weekly-mail`                | The medium isn't a standard word                      | Use `utm_medium=email`                                                |
| `x / post` or `linkedin / organic-social` | Social medium spelled differently                     | Use `utm_medium=social`                                               |
| `partner / link`                          | Unusual referral medium                               | Use `utm_medium=referral` or `affiliate`                              |
| `(not set) / (not set)`                   | Events without a session start, or sent from a server | Check your tag fires on page load; see [(not set)](/glossary/not-set) |

GA4 recognises mediums like `email`, `social`, `referral`, `affiliate`, `cpc`, `display` and `organic`. Stick to these in your [UTM links](/guides/utm-links).

## Past data

Fixing your links changes new visits only. For old ones, you can create a custom channel group with your own rules (Admin → Data display → Channel groups), which also applies to past data in reports.

<InSimpleDash>
  SimpleDash shows Unassigned as **Unknown** in the Channels tab, and the raw sources one tab over,
  so you can spot a badly tagged newsletter at a glance.
</InSimpleDash>
