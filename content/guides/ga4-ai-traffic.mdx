Visits from AI assistants show up in Google Analytics 4 as referrals from their websites, such as `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai` and `claude.ai`. GA4 also groups many of them under an **AI Assistant** channel. To see them all in one place, filter Traffic acquisition by source, or create a custom channel group with a short rule.

## Quick look: filter by source

<Steps>
<Step>

Open Reports → Acquisition → **Traffic acquisition**.

</Step>
<Step>

Change the table's first column to **Session source** (the dropdown above the first column).

</Step>
<Step>

In the table's search box, type `chatgpt`, then `perplexity`, `claude`, `gemini` and `copilot` one at a time.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The sources to look for

| Assistant               | Source in GA4                                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| ChatGPT                 | `chatgpt.com`, older visits `chat.openai.com`        |
| Perplexity              | `perplexity.ai`, `www.perplexity.ai`                 |
| Claude                  | `claude.ai`                                          |
| Gemini                  | `gemini.google.com` (older: `bard.google.com`)       |
| Copilot                 | `copilot.microsoft.com`                              |
| DeepSeek, Grok, Meta AI | `chat.deepseek.com`, `grok.com`, `meta.ai`           |
| Others                  | `chat.mistral.ai`, `you.com`, `phind.com`, `poe.com` |

ChatGPT also adds `utm_source=chatgpt.com` to many links it shows, so those visits show `chatgpt.com` as their source even when the browser doesn't pass a referrer.

## See them all at once: an AI channel group [#ai-channel-group]

GA4's default channels include **AI Assistant** for many of these. To make sure all of them are grouped, including newer assistants, create your own channel group:

<Steps>
<Step>

Admin → Data display → **Channel groups** → **Create new channel group**. Name it, for example, "Channels with AI".

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Add new channel**, name it "AI assistants", and add the condition **Source** · **matches regex** ·

```text
chatgpt|openai|perplexity|claude|gemini|bard|copilot|deepseek|grok|meta\.ai|mistral|you\.com|phind|poe\.com
```

</Step>
<Step>

Save, then use **Reorder** to move "AI assistants" **above Referral**, or GA4 sorts these visits into Referral first.

</Step>
<Step>

In Traffic acquisition, switch the first column to your new channel group.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What GA4 can't see

- **Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode** count as Google organic search. Google doesn't report them separately in GA4; their clicks are in Search Console's performance report, together with other Google results.
- **Apps that hide the referrer.** Some visits from AI apps arrive without a referrer and show up as **Direct**. [Why direct traffic is high](/guides/ga4-direct-traffic).
- **Answers without a click.** If an assistant mentions your site but nobody clicks, there's no visit to count.
