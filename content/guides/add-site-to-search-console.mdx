To add your site to Google Search Console, go to search.google.com/search-console, add a **Domain** property for your whole domain and verify it with a DNS record, or add a **URL prefix** property and verify it with your existing Google Analytics tag. Data starts appearing within a few days.

## Domain or URL prefix?

|             | Domain property                                                                                     | URL prefix property                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Covers      | Every subdomain and protocol: `mysite.com`, `www.mysite.com`, `blog.mysite.com`, `http` and `https` | Only addresses that start with exactly what you enter, like `https://www.mysite.com/` |
| Verify with | A DNS record at your domain provider                                                                | An HTML file, a meta tag, your GA4 tag or Google Tag Manager                          |
| Best for    | Most sites                                                                                          | When you can't change DNS                                                             |

Choose **Domain** if you can: it sees all your traffic in one place.

## Add a Domain property

<Steps>
<Step>

Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console), choose **Add property**, and enter your domain without `https://` or `www`, for example `mysite.com`.

</Step>
<Step>

Google shows a **TXT record** like `google-site-verification=…`. If your domain is at a provider Google recognises (Cloudflare, for example), it can offer to add the record for you: follow its button and sign in to your provider.

</Step>
<Step>

Otherwise, copy the record, open your domain provider's DNS settings, and add a **TXT** record for the root of your domain (often written as `@`) with that value.

</Step>
<Step>

Back in Search Console, choose **Verify**. DNS changes can take a few minutes to a few hours; if it fails, wait and try again.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Add a URL prefix property (with your GA4 tag)

1. Choose **Add property** → **URL prefix**, and enter your full address, for example `https://www.mysite.com/`.
2. Choose **Google Analytics** as the verification method. It works if your homepage has the GA4 tag and your Google account can edit that GA4 property.
3. Choose **Verify**.

## What happens next

- **Data takes a few days** to appear, and Search Console then shows up to 16 months.
- **Submit your sitemap:** Indexing → Sitemaps → enter `sitemap.xml` (or your sitemap's address).
- **Look at Performance → Search results** to see clicks, impressions, click rate and position for your searches and pages.
