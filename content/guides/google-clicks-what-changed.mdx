When your clicks from Google change, Search Console's **compare** mode shows which pages and searches are behind it. For each, look at position and impressions: a fall in position means your ranking changed; a fall in impressions with the same position means fewer people searched.

## Compare two periods

<Steps>
<Step>

Search Console → **Performance → Search results** → the date filter → **Compare**, for example the last 28 days against the 28 days before.

</Step>
<Step>

Open the **Pages** tab and sort by **Clicks difference**. The pages that lost or gained the most are at the top.

</Step>
<Step>

Click one page, then the **Queries** tab, to see which searches changed for it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Read each change

| What you see                                         | What changed                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Position fell, impressions fell                      | Your ranking dropped                                                     |
| Position the same, impressions fell                  | Fewer people searched (seasonal, or interest moved)                      |
| Position the same, impressions the same, clicks fell | Fewer people clicked: a new Google feature above you, or a better result |
| Clicks fell only on a few days                       | Something temporary: an outage, a holiday                                |

## Keep to the facts

These numbers tell you _what_ changed. Before acting on a guess about _why_, check what you changed on the page, look at the results page yourself, and give it time: rankings move around for weeks after Google's updates.
