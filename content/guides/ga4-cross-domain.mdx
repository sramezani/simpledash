If your marketing site and your app or shop are on **different domains** (for example `mysite.com` and `mysiteapp.com`), set up cross-domain measurement in your GA4 data stream, so a visitor moving between them stays one visit with its original source. **Subdomains** of the same domain (`mysite.com` and `app.mysite.com`) work without it.

## Do you need it?

| Your setup                                              | Need cross-domain?                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mysite.com` and `app.mysite.com`                       | No: subdomains share GA4's cookie automatically                             |
| `mysite.com` and `mysite.io`                            | Yes                                                                         |
| `mysite.com` and a checkout on Stripe, PayPal or Paddle | No: use [unwanted referrals](/guides/ga4-unwanted-referrals-stripe) instead |

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

Put the **same** GA4 tag (same Measurement ID) on both domains.

</Step>
<Step>

In GA4, open Admin → Data streams → your web stream → **Configure tag settings** → **Configure your domains**.

</Step>
<Step>

Add a condition for each domain, for example **Contains** `mysite.com` and **Contains** `mysite.io`. Save.

</Step>
</Steps>

GA4 then adds a short `_gl=` parameter to links between the domains, which carries the visitor across.

## Check it works

Click a link from one domain to the other: the address should include `_gl=`. In GA4's Realtime report, the visit keeps its original source instead of showing your other domain as a referral.

<InSimpleDash>
  With both domains on one GA4 property, SimpleDash shows them as one site, with sources and landing
  pages across both.
</InSimpleDash>
