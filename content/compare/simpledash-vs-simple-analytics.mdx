The names are close, but the tools aren't. **Simple Analytics** is a privacy-first replacement for Google Analytics, made in the Netherlands, with its own script. **SimpleDash** keeps Google Analytics 4 and shows its data simply. One replaces GA4; the other makes GA4 easy.

## Side by side

|                                            | Simple Analytics | SimpleDash                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tracking                                   | Its own script   | None: reads your GA4                                                           |
| Cookies                                    | No cookies       | GA4's (with your consent banner)                                               |
| Your existing GA4 history                  | Can import it    | Read directly                                                                  |
| Data location                              | EU               | GA4's data stays at Google; SimpleDash keeps only short-lived copies of totals |
| Public stats page                          | Yes              | Yes                                                                            |
| Search Console and Stripe on the same page | No               | Yes (Pro)                                                                      |
| Open source                                | No               | Yes (AGPL-3.0)                                                                 |

Check [Simple Analytics' site](https://www.simpleanalytics.com) for its current plans and features.

## Choose Simple Analytics if

you want privacy-first analytics without cookies, from a European company, and you're ready to replace GA4.

## Choose SimpleDash if

you already have GA4 and want its numbers in plain words on one page, with your history, and nothing new on your site.
