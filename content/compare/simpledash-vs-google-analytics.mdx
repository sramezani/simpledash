SimpleDash isn't a rival to Google Analytics 4: it reads your GA4 data and shows it on one simple page, in plain words. GA4 keeps collecting the data and stays the place for deep analysis. SimpleDash is for the weekly questions: how many came, from where, and did anyone sign up or pay.

## The short answer

- **Use GA4** to collect data, and for funnels, explorations, audiences and anything detailed.
- **Use SimpleDash** to check your numbers in seconds, get a Monday email and share a public stats page, without learning GA4's menus.
- **Most people use both**: SimpleDash every week, GA4 when they need to dig.

## Where GA4 is better

- **Depth.** Explorations, funnels, path analysis, segments and audiences.
- **Customisation.** Custom events, dimensions, metrics and reports.
- **Advertising.** Google Ads integration and attribution models.

## Where SimpleDash is better

- **Speed to an answer.** One page, no menus, numbers in plain words, each compared with the period before.
- **Honest defaults.** No fake zeros: missing data says so, and the part of the chart still filling up is dashed.
- **Sharing.** A public stats page for building in public, and a Monday email.
- **One view of your business.** With Pro, Search Console and Stripe revenue next to your visitors.

## Can SimpleDash change my GA4?

No. Access is read-only. SimpleDash can't change settings, add users or delete data. [What SimpleDash can see](/docs/google-access).
