**DataFast** is analytics built for founders, with its own tracking script and a focus on which channels bring revenue. **SimpleDash** reads the Google Analytics 4 data you already have, and on Pro adds Stripe revenue, MRR and sales by source from GA4. DataFast replaces your tracking; SimpleDash keeps GA4.

## Side by side

| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tracking                        | Its own script                               | None: reads your GA4                                                       |
| Your existing GA4 history       | Not read                                     | Read directly                                                              |

| Free plan                       | Check DataFast's site                        | Yes, all your sites                                                        |

Check [DataFast's site](https://datafa.st) for its current plans and features.

## How revenue differs

DataFast connects payments to individual visitors through its own script, so it can say which channel a payment came from. SimpleDash never tracks individual visitors: it shows Stripe's totals as they are, and sales by source only where GA4 itself recorded them. Nothing is matched or estimated, so some sales stay unattributed rather than guessed.

## Choose DataFast if

you're happy to add a new tracking script and want revenue tied to visitors and channels.

## Choose SimpleDash if

you want to keep GA4, add nothing to your site, and see exact Stripe totals next to your traffic.
