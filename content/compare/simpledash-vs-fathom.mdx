Fathom Analytics is a privacy-first alternative to Google Analytics, with its own cookie-free script. SimpleDash takes the opposite route: it keeps Google Analytics 4 and shows its data on one simple page. Choose Fathom to replace GA4; choose SimpleDash to keep GA4 and make it easy.

## Side by side

|                                            | Fathom                    | SimpleDash                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Tracking                                   | Its own script            | None: reads your GA4             |
| Cookies                                    | Cookie-free               | GA4's (with your consent banner) |
| Your existing GA4 history                  | Can import it             | Read directly                    |
| Open source                                | No                        | Yes (AGPL-3.0)                   |
| Simple dashboard                           | Yes                       | Yes                              |
| Public stats page                          | Yes, shareable dashboards | Yes                              |
| Weekly email                               | Email reports             | The Monday email (Pro)           |
| Search Console and Stripe on the same page | No                        | Yes (Pro)                        |
| Free plan                                  | No (a trial)              | Yes, all your sites              |

Check [Fathom's site](https://usefathom.com) for its current plans and features.

## Choose Fathom if

- you want to leave Google Analytics for a privacy-first tool,
- you want a polished, cookie-free product with support behind it.

## Choose SimpleDash if

- you're happy with GA4 collecting data and want it readable,
- you don't want a second script on your site,
- you want Search Console and Stripe next to your visitors (Pro), or a free plan for many sites.
