If GA4's reports feel like too much, there are three ways to get a simpler dashboard: build one inside GA4 with its new built-in dashboard, build one in Looker Studio, or use a ready-made dashboard that reads your GA4 for you, like SimpleDash. Pick by one question: do you want to build it, or just read it?

## The options at a glance

| Option                          | You build it?           | All your sites on one screen? | Cost                                                                       |

## 1. GA4's built-in dashboard

New in 2026: GA4 can now show your own cards on one page. You drag score cards, tables, line, bar, donut and funnel charts onto a grid, and publish the result to the Reports menu.

- **Best for:** people who know GA4's metric names and want one page inside GA4.
- **Trade-offs:** you build every card yourself and need the Editor or Administrator role; up to 15 cards on a standard property; no segments or card-level comparisons; one property at a time. It still uses GA4's words.
- **Cost:** free.
- [How to use GA4's built-in dashboard](/guides/ga4-built-in-dashboard)

## 2. GA4's own reports, customised

GA4 lets you edit its report collections, hide reports you don't use, and pin a few cards to the home page.

- **Best for:** people who already know GA4 and want fewer clicks.
- **Trade-offs:** still GA4's words and menus; customising takes time.
- **Cost:** free.

## 3. Looker Studio

Google's free report builder, with a GA4 connector and many templates.

- **Best for:** custom reports, blending GA4 with other data, agency reports.
- **Trade-offs:** you build and maintain it (a copied template still needs connecting and fixing when it breaks); busy reports load slowly and can hit GA4's API limits.
- **Cost:** free (a paid Pro version exists).
- [SimpleDash vs Looker Studio](/compare/simpledash-vs-looker-studio)

## 4. SimpleDash

A ready dashboard on top of GA4: sign in with Google and your numbers are on one page, in plain words.

- **Best for:** founders and small sites who want answers in seconds: visitors, sources, top pages and signups, every GA4 site one click away, a Monday email and a public stats page.
- **Trade-offs:** fixed cards; no funnels, journeys or custom reports.
- **Cost:** free for all your sites, with no limit; Pro <Price plan="pro" interval="month" /> a month for 12 months of history, the Monday email, Google search insights and revenue from Stripe.
- [SimpleDash vs Google Analytics](/compare/simpledash-vs-google-analytics)

## 5. Other ready-made GA4 dashboards

A few other tools read your GA4 and show it more simply, each with its own focus. In their own words (checked on Nov 8, 2026):

| Tool                                         | What it says it is                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [MetriBee](https://metribee.com/)            | A free, open-source GA4 dashboard: every site on one screen, realtime spike alerts, funnels and journeys |
| [BetterGA](https://better-ga.com/)           | A one-screen Google Analytics dashboard                                                                  |
| [OwlInsight](https://owlinsight.dev/)        | A simple view of GA4: trends, channels, top pages, countries, browsers and devices                       |
| [SitePulse](https://sitepulseanalytics.com/) | A simple GA4 and Search Console dashboard for agencies and teams, with client reports                    |
| [Ramprt](https://ramprt.io/)                 | A free GA4 dashboard for ad-funded publishers, focused on AI traffic and crawlers                        |

Check each tool's own page for prices and what it can read. All of these, like SimpleDash, need GA4 to be collecting data on your site.

## 6. WordPress plugins

If your site runs on WordPress, a plugin can show GA4's main numbers inside the WordPress dashboard. **Site Kit by Google** is Google's own free plugin, and also shows Search Console; **MonsterInsights** has a free version and paid plans.

- **Best for:** one WordPress site, checked from its own admin.
- **Trade-offs:** WordPress only, one site at a time, and a plugin to keep updated.

## 7. Business dashboard tools

Tools that connect GA4 among dozens of sources suit teams that want marketing, sales and finance in one place. They're usually more than a small site needs, and priced for teams.
