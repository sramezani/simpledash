If GA4's interface feels like too much, there are three main ways to get a simpler dashboard: customise GA4's own reports, build one in Looker Studio, or use a ready-made dashboard like SimpleDash. The right pick depends on whether you'd rather build or just read.

## 1. GA4's own reports, customised

GA4 lets you edit its report collections, hide reports you don't use, and pin a few cards to the home page.

- **Best for:** people who already know GA4 and want fewer clicks.
- **Trade-offs:** still GA4's words and menus; customising takes time.
- **Cost:** free.

## 2. Looker Studio

Google's free report builder, with a GA4 connector and many templates.

- **Best for:** custom reports, blending GA4 with other data, agency reports.
- **Trade-offs:** you build and maintain it; busy reports can hit GA4's API limits.
- **Cost:** free (a paid Pro version exists).
- [SimpleDash vs Looker Studio](/compare/simpledash-vs-looker-studio)

## 3. SimpleDash

A ready dashboard on top of GA4: sign in with Google and your numbers are on one page.

- **Best for:** founders and small sites who want answers in seconds, plain words, a Monday email and a public stats page.
- **Trade-offs:** fixed cards; no funnels or custom reports.
- **Cost:** free for all your sites; Pro <Price plan="pro" interval="month" /> a month for full history, the Monday email, Search Console and Stripe.
- [SimpleDash vs Google Analytics](/compare/simpledash-vs-google-analytics)

## 4. Other dashboard tools

Business dashboard tools (many connect to GA4 among dozens of sources) suit teams that want marketing, sales and finance in one place. They're usually more than a small site needs, and priced for teams.

## How to choose

| You want                             | Pick                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Full control and don't mind building | Looker Studio                                                                              |
| Answers now, no building             | SimpleDash                                                                                 |
| To stay inside GA4                   | Customised GA4 reports                                                                     |
| To leave Google entirely             | A tracker with its own script: [compare them](/compare/simple-analytics-for-indie-hackers) |
